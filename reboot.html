Pinterest for Gamers

Personal Interest

When I look up a build, an outfit, or a walkthrough, I bounce between Pinterest, YouTube, Discord, and wiki pages. I lose my place every time I switch apps.

  • I hunt Pinterest for outfit ideas from armor sets and Minecraft builds.
  • I get stuck in story games like Zelda: Majora's Mask with no simple step-by-step help.
  • I wanted one place for inspiration, builds, and guides.
How Might We

How might we give gamers one place for inspiration and guidance, so they never get stuck or jump between apps?

The Big Idea

Pinterest, built for gamers.

For You

Posts from other gamers, picked only from what you like.

Organized

Boards and sub-boards keep every idea easy to find.

Guided

Step-by-step help, so you never leave the app to get unstuck.

Competitors

Gamers use four apps, and each one does one job.

I compared the apps gamers told me they use, based on each app's own pages. Pinterest sorts ideas. HallOfFramed shows them off. Reddit and Steam help when you're stuck. Reboot puts all of it in one app, made only for games.

Focus Group & Results
Focus Group

I watched three gamers use Reboot together in one session and let them talk.

Kathy
  • Make Explore easier to see so new users know where to start.
  • Add inspiration and "stuck" categories to guide unsure users.
  • Round the corners for a more consistent, balanced feel.
Xally
  • Reduce heavy borders, improve spacing between images.
  • Add Step 1 / Step 2 guidance so the next action is obvious.
  • Highlight selected boards, add a favorite icon.
  • Move main navigation to the bottom of the screen.
Isabella
  • Make strokes bigger. Some areas were hard to see.
  • Use a heart icon on the For You page instead of a pin icon.
  • Center the title at the top of the screen.
More Feedback · The For You Feed

For You should mean for you.

A friend tried the prototype. His feed kept showing cute posts and PC hardware, and he only cares about creative screenshots and guides. So Explore is now a For You page. It only shows what you say you like.

1

You pick

The first time you open Reboot, you choose what you like: creative shots, guides, builds, or outfits. Nothing else shows up until you add it.

2

It learns from you

Every heart, repost, and save brings more of the same. Posts you scroll past don't get pushed.

3

You can say no

Tap Not for me to hide a whole topic, like cute posts or PC setups.

The rule: if you didn't pick it, save it, or heart something like it, it stays out of For You.

More Feedback · The Approach

Less pinboard, more timeline.

The same friend said Reboot should feel less like a pinboard and more like a feed of real players. A lot of gamers don't use Pinterest at all. So now every post comes from a person, with a caption and replies, instead of an anonymous pin. Reboot keeps its name.

Before · like Pinterest

A grid of pins

  • Images without a person attached.
  • Saving to boards is the main action.
  • Works best for artsy browsing.
Now · a timeline of posts

Posts from gamers

  • Every post has a person, a caption and a time.
  • Reply, repost, heart, and save.
  • A Following tab next to For You.
  • Guides can be threads, one step per post.
What stays: Boards are still there for saving and sharing ideas. Only the feed changes.

Conclusions
What I Learned

Reboot had to show enough inspiration without making the app hard to use. I let tester feedback decide the bottom nav bar and the rounded corners.

What's Next

First, build the For You feed into the prototype: posts from other gamers, a first-open picker, and a Not for me button. Then in-app sharing, so groups can work on one board. Then community features, so gamers hang out in Reboot.
