How I Work

The same process, every time.

Four steps, in the same order, on every project on this site. The tools change from one to the next; the sequence does not.

01

Listen

Interviews, surveys, and a hard look at what competitors already get right or wrong.

  • User Interviews
  • Surveys
  • Competitive Audit

02

Define

Turn scattered findings into personas, a sharp problem statement, and the flows that solve it.

  • Personas
  • How Might We
  • Task Flows

03

Build

Paper to low fidelity to high, with a design system so the hundredth screen still matches the first.

  • Wireframes
  • Design Systems
  • Prototypes

04

Prove

Put it in front of real people, watch where they stall, then change the design instead of the user.

  • Usability Testing
  • A/B Testing
  • Iteration

About

I'd rather be wrong in a usability session than in production.

Spotify and Nuance each went through two rounds of usability testing before I called them done, and every project here was rebuilt at least once on the strength of what testers said, because good instincts still have to survive contact with real people.

I also take work the full distance myself: research, UI, and a working, branded prototype. I work in Figma, Framer, Webflow, Photoshop, Illustrator, and After Effects, and I write my own front end when a project calls for it. This site is the second kind: every page here is hand-written HTML and CSS.
